Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy explains how the game Angry Sling ("the App", developer Josiah Shaw) handles information.

Information we collect

  • Sign in with Apple (optional): if you sign in, we receive an Apple user identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name and email (which may be a private relay address). We use it only to save your progress and show your name on the leaderboard.
  • Game data: best scores and save data, stored on our backend (Supabase, United States) when you are signed in. Without signing in, data stays on your device.
  • Purchases: purchases are processed by Apple. We receive transaction notifications from Apple (product, transaction ID, expiry, refund status) to grant VIP / Remove Ads. We never receive your payment details.
  • Advertising: the App shows ads via Google AdMob for non-VIP users. AdMob may collect device identifiers (IDFA, only if you allow tracking via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt), IP address, and ad interaction data. See Google's policy. EU/UK users are asked for consent where required.

How we use it

To provide gameplay, leaderboards, cloud save, purchases and ads. We do not sell personal data.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. To delete your account and data, contact us (below); we will delete it within 30 days. You can also stop using Sign in with Apple in iOS Settings › Apple ID › Sign in with Apple.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change.
